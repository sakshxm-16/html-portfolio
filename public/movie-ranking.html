The Best Series According To Saksham

My Top 5 Series

  1. Dark (2019)

    A German science fiction series that follows the story of a missing child and the dark secrets of a small town.
    It's a mind-bending series that explores time travel, family secrets, and the consequences of our actions.
    The show has a complex narrative that requires close attention, but it's worth it for the payoff.

  2. Game of Thrones (2011-2019)

    A fantasy series that follows the story of several noble families vying for control of the Iron Throne.
    It's a epic tale of power, politics, and the struggle for survival in a medieval-inspired world.
    The show has a large ensemble cast and a complex plot that keeps viewers engaged.

  3. Demon Slayer : Kimetsu no Yaiba (2019-2020)

    A Japanese animated series that follows the story of a young boy who becomes a demon slayer to avenge his family and find a cure for his sister.
    It's a visually stunning series that combines action, adventure, and emotional depth.
    The show has a strong protagonist and a compelling narrative that keeps viewers engaged.

  4. The OA (2016-2017)

    A mysterious series that follows the story of a young woman who returns from the dead and the impact it has on her family and community.
    It's a thought-provoking series that explores themes of identity, spirituality, and the nature of reality.
    The show has a unique visual style and a compelling narrative that keeps viewers engaged.

  5. Archive 81 (2020)

    A supernatural series that follows the story of a young woman who discovers a hidden world of spirits and the impact it has on her life.
    It's a gripping series that explores themes of fate, destiny, and the power of belief.
    The show has a unique atmosphere and a compelling narrative that keeps viewers engaged.
